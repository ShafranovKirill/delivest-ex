# F01 marketing reports

`Marketing.Report.request/2` reads a cached policy snapshot or creates a pending request. Filters are an inclusive UTC issue-date range of at most 90 days and an optional reward-issuing branch. The authenticated Marketing wrapper checks staff scope before calling it. `compare/2` coalesces up to ten ready policy reports and materializes a separate, order-deduplicated result. No request handler scans order history.

The shared `marketing.report` Background task processes at most 50 issues or 50 customer orders per step. Issue and order cursors, a request timestamp, and the Background generation/job-owner fence prevent an old worker from publishing over a newer request. A ready report is refreshed after 24 hours. When refresh starts, the request becomes pending before its stored links are deleted in pages of 50; comparison snapshots using it become stale before deletion. The UI displays pending and `as_of`. Each order is counted once within one policy and once in a comparison, even when several issues or policies overlap.

Linked orders are observed completed orders whose completion transition falls after an issue's stored `issued_at` and no later than its stored `attribution_until`. Net revenue subtracts succeeded refunds. This is observational attribution, not proven incremental sales. Daily series use the issue date and reward-issuing branch for both issue and linked-order metrics. An order placed at another branch remains linked when policy scope permits; the link also records its separate return-order branch. Comparison `return_branches` breaks down deduplicated orders by return-order branch. `in_app_available` means a site card was available; `push_provider_accepted` means the push gateway accepted a request. Neither is confirmed delivery. SMS is unconfigured, so `delivered` and `sms_cost` remain unknown. Bonus issue points are known; exact redeemed points and campaign bonus cost remain unknown because the current ledger has no allocation from a redemption or expiration to a specific issued reward.

Comparison data includes each member snapshot timestamp. Its overall linked-order/revenue counts deduplicate order IDs across policies. Per-policy rows may overlap; adding their revenue would double count. A stale or disabled feature has no new optional calculation. The full F04 journey funnel and causal uplift are outside this report.

The exclusion count uses the policy's current reward-issuing branch, including when the excluded cycle order was placed elsewhere. Exclusion rows do not store the reward branch at exclusion time. Editing a policy's reward branch can therefore change the historical branch-filtered exclusion count; the report cannot reconstruct the earlier branch from these rows.
